Our Story

Built from driftwood and stubbornness.

Driftwood started as a coffee cart on the Waterfront with one grinder and a lot of hope. Locals kept coming back, so we found a little corner with a view of the lagoon and built our counter from wood that washed up on the shore.

We still roast every bean ourselves, we still know most of our regulars by name, and we still think the best mornings start slowly.
