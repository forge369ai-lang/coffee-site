We built the counter from wood the lagoon washed up. The coffee took a little longer.

Driftwood started in 2018 as a coffee cart on the Waterfront: one grinder, one kettle and a lot of hope. Locals kept coming back, so we found a small corner with a view of the Heads and moved in.

We still roast every bean ourselves, we still know most of our regulars by name, and we still think the best mornings start slowly. Come early for the light on the water.
