Why I'm doing this

This year I made a decision to change my life. My health, my habits, the way I show up for my family.

And somewhere along the way, it stopped being just about the gym. As I've been giving my life to Christ, I've felt a strong calling to give back to the community that's given so much to me.

Christmas is hard for a lot of families. Some parents are standing at the till deciding what to put back. Some are putting in R50 of petrol to get through the week. Some kids won't have anything to open on Christmas morning.

So on Saturday 12 December I'm doing 3,000 pushups on a live stream. Every rand you give goes straight to families in Knysna and Sedgefield.

I can't do this alone. Every pushup is a prayer, and every donation is someone's Christmas.

Where your money goes

Real needs. Real people. Right here.

No admin fees, no salaries. We go to the shops and fuel stations of Knysna and Sedgefield and quietly pay for what families need.

Groceries

Paying the till for parents doing the weekly shop, so nothing has to go back on the shelf.

Fuel

Filling up the tank for people who are getting by on a few litres at a time.

Clothes

Clothes and school basics for kids who need them, bought with their parents.

Christmas presents

Toys and gifts so every child we meet has something to open on Christmas morning.

Questions

Good to know.

Is my donation safe?

Yes. All donations go through BackaBuddy, a South African crowdfunding platform built for exactly this. The money goes into the campaign fund and is used only to help families.

How do you choose who to help?

We go to shopping centres and fuel stations around Knysna and Sedgefield and look for people who are clearly stretching every rand. We pay quietly and respectfully. No forms, no conditions.

Will people be filmed?

We film the moments so you can see your money at work, but we always ask first. If someone says no, we only show the trolley, the till or the receipt. Children are never shown.

Can I give goods instead of money?

Absolutely. Groceries, toys, clothes or fuel vouchers all help. Send Jay a WhatsApp on 061 582 6878 to arrange drop-off.

Can I watch the pushups live?

Yes. The stream runs on Instagram (@jay.steenkamp) and YouTube (@jaysteenkamp) on Saturday 12 December. The start time will be posted here and on socials.

What if you raise more than R5,000?

Then we help more families. Anything raised above the goal goes to the same cause, and the full spend is shown on this page.
